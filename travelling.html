#TRAVELLING
Personal Conceptual Project

This project is a personal exploration of turning some of my favourite travel memories into a collection of stamp-inspired designs. I used my own photographs from places I loved being in — including Miami, Roatán, Quintana Roo, Madrid and Stockholm, and transformed them through a mix of photo editing, layering, colour adjustments, textures, noise and graphic elements. I wanted each stamp to feel like a small piece of a place and a memory, while pushing the original photographs into something more stylised and unexpected.

A big part of the project was creating a consistent visual language across all the different locations. Although each city has its own colours, atmosphere and imagery, the repeated stamp format, typography, textures and bold colour treatments help bring the whole collection together. I really enjoyed playing with the balance between photography and graphic design, making the images feel slightly vintage, printed and imperfect while still keeping them bright and eye-catching. The final collection feels playful and distinctive, while still working as one cohesive series.
