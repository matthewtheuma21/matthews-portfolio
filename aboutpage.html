I hold a Bachelor of Arts (Hons) in Graphic Design from MCAST Mosta, where I developed a strong foundation across a range of design disciplines, working with different outputs and mediums including editorial, branding, photography, and digital design.

My approach to design is rooted in clarity, structure, and visual impact. I am drawn to the power of simplicity, but for me, simplicity is never about making something feel empty or stripped back. I enjoy giving simple ideas character (introducing bold, unexpected, and sensorial elements that make a piece feel distinctive and memorable. Whether restrained or expressive, I aim for every design choice to have a purpose and contribute to the overall experience.

Throughout my work, I focus on creating designs that are both purposeful and engaging, with particular attention to composition, hierarchy, and narrative. My studies have also encouraged me to think critically about design as a form of communication (something that can inform, evoke, and create a connection, rather than simply exist for aesthetics).

I also genuinely enjoy sharing what I know. Teaching, explaining, and helping someone understand something I love is something I find incredibly rewarding. I believe that design is an ongoing exchange of ideas, and that there is always something to learn, share, or see from a different perspective.

Beyond design, I value growth through new experiences and challenges. Travelling has played a significant role in shaping my perspective, allowing me to engage with different cultures and visual environments across Europe and the Americas. These experiences continue to influence the way I see, think, and create. Some of the projects shown here include work completed for clients.
